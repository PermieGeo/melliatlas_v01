**Requirement:** [SRC-R01 and SRC-R02](specs/active/subspecs/source-ingestion.md).

This register records inspectable publications, not a machine-ingestion authorization or a live source catalog. The links below were inspected through publicly indexed page/PDF views. ## Operator follow-up before enabling a source

1. Verify the publisher's identity and current official domain independently; record a dated page or response supporting authorized collection and downstream display. Where terms are unclear, seek written permission or a clearly applicable published license.
2. Check `robots.txt`, any `Terms of Use` or copyright notice, the allowed URL scope, attachment ownership, pagination, acceptable rate/cadence and whether the notice may be retained and redistributed. Record the reviewer's name and UTC time.
3. Test one permitted document manually from an authorized environment: HTTP status, redirect chain, content type, maximum size, dates, encoding and stable canonical URL. Document failures without expanding allowlists automatically.
4. Establish a least-privilege, authenticated operator workflow for registration and revocation. Review credentials and audit trails must not be exposed by public source endpoints.
5. Only then set a narrow host/path policy and disabled-by-default fetch configuration, verify the restriction tests and obtain explicit approval for the first scheduled request.

## Policy and science cautions

- The reference grant proposal includes a three-day notice assumption. A [2026-03-27 Rosselkhoznadzor communication](https://www.garant.ru/products/ipo/prime/doc/413900975/) reports that the amended Article 16 of Federal Law No. 490-FZ (effective 2026-03-01) generally specifies notification **between ten and five calendar days before application**, with an emergency exception. Verify the current official legislation independently before implementing legal compliance labels; do not infer lawfulness from the date fields of a notice.
- The names of pesticides and hazard classes in source notices are **publisher claims**, not independently validated toxicity or safety assessments. Missing notices do not prove absence of agricultural treatment.
- The source project suggests a Saturn-related API and region identifier. Neither its undocumented endpoint nor its automated-access rights were verified in this research. Do not add it to approved sources.

## Next implementation boundaries

1. **SRC-01 / Batch A, step 5:** Authenticated source administration with reviewed decisions, revocation and no arbitrary URLs. This register alone does not authorize approval.
2. **SRC-01 / Batch B, step 6:** Restricted HTTP fetcher with DNS/address validation, redirect revalidation, strict size/MIME/time limits and deterministic denial tests. It must remain disconnected from automatic jobs until access approval.

## Priority integration: FGIS Saturn Beekeeper Portal (2026-11-01)

The FGIS Saturn Beekeeper Portal is the first integration priority, ahead of municipal adapters. Its public, unauthenticated **human-facing** view of proposed pesticide applications is described by Rosselkhoznadzor (2025-03-26): https://base.garant.ru/411752129/ . The agency's 2026-03-27 communication lists `https://bee-saturn.ru` as the portal: https://base.garant.ru/414000975/ . A separate institutional SE-API integration guide exists at https://fgis-saturn.ru/api/seapi_%D0%B1%D1%8B%D1%81%D1%82%D1%80%D1%8B%D0%B9_%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%80%D1%82.v2022.12.21_0900.pdf ; it does not establish the public portal's machine-readable export contract.

**Candidate-only settings:** `https://bee-saturn.ru/backend/beemaster/planapplybees`, `POST`, `subject: 55` from the earlier prototype. These identifiers and the current response schema have **not** been independently verified for production access. The configuration can be edited, versioned and used in offline request-builder tests, but is stored with `pending` permission and cannot initiate requests.

**Required before a live adapter:** inspect the official current endpoint and authentication behavior; confirm terms for automatic reads, permitted polling and downstream display; document real request/response shapes and pagination; set operator-reviewed allowlists; test TLS/DNS/redirect/size/time defenses; reconcile source status and actual field geometries. Do not treat the separately documented SE-API submission methods as a public read interface. Records must retain the original source URL and uncertainty, and historical plans must not be shown as current treatments.
